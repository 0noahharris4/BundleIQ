Association strength is measured with standard market-basket metrics: support (how often a pair appears together), confidence (given the first product, how often the second follows), and lift (how much more often the pair occurs than random chance -- a lift of 1 means no relationship; this table only shows pairs above a minimum support and a lift greater than 1.15). Product names are matched case/whitespace-insensitively, so "Eggs", "EGGS", and " eggs" are counted as one product. Seasons are calendar-based (Winter = Dec–Feb, Spring = Mar–May, Summer = Jun–Aug, Fall = Sep–Nov) and only appear once an order-date column is provided. All analysis runs on the file you uploaded and is not stored.
